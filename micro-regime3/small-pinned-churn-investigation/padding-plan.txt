1. IMPLEMENTATION -- the toggle and the form problem.

1.1 The change: every ARM RESULT allocation of l < 410 doubles is
allocated at 410 and returned as an l-slice (VS.unsafeTake keeps the
padded ForeignPtr; semantics unchanged). Setup vectors stay unpadded
(two long-lived objects, no churn). Only the two main-set shapes have
sub-threshold results (cnn-slice-c32 at 288, cnn-L1-6x6-c1 at 324 --
old plan 1.1's size table), but the padding applies by size, not by
shape list, so stride-class populations are covered wherever they
qualify.

1.2 The form problem: builders allocate through VS.fromListN,
VS.generate, VS.create and unsafeBackpermute-style combinators that
take no allocation length. Rewriting a builder to create+fill changes
compiled code, and micro's whole point is that its arms are faithful
replicas compared against each other -- so the toggle must not be a
per-builder rewrite that only the padded state exercises. Design, on
MixedLoad.hs's precedent: route BOTH toggle states through the same
allocation form, with the length the only difference -- a top-level
constant `padSmall :: Bool` and `padTo l = if padSmall && l < 410 then
410 else l`, and padded counterparts of the few result constructors
(inventory them by grepping the arm builders; expect fromListN,
generate, create/unsafeNew, backpermute) used unconditionally. The
A/B is then three binaries: run15 (old forms), newform-unpadded
(padSmall = False), newform-padded (padSmall = True) -- the first pair
isolates the form change, the second the padding. Toggle by editing
the constant and rebuilding, binaries copied aside (the toggle-A/B
recipe in horde-ad CLAUDE.md).

1.3 Correctness: micro's `check` must stay green on both new binaries
(the padded slice must be indistinguishable to every consumer), and
--lint/--check-doc untouched. A padded result's VS.length is l; only
its ForeignPtr capacity differs.

2. MEASUREMENTS, cheapest-refutation first. Registered leans are
   judged, not remembered.

2.1 THE 32M MATCHED-DOSE CLASS CELL (the 0.2 discriminator; item 13
ran it at 1G only). Victim conv1d-24/list at -A32m, fixed-n
differencing with poison-alone runs subtracted, run15 binary, poisons
cnn-slice-c32/list and cnn-L1-24x24-c1/list at -n 200k/400k each (~8
processes, ~25 min). Lean: the class split holds at 32m as it does in
ReproSmall (sub-threshold well above own-group), which is what gives
padding its payoff; the opposite result re-scopes tasks 2.4-2.5's
predictions downward and is itself a finding for the GHC comment.

2.2 FORM-CHANGE NULL: newform-unpadded against run15, interleaved
pairs on vgg-14-c512-k3 (all six measured arms) plus list on both
small shapes, at the default area (~20 min). Lean: within the drift
band, most arms under 1.5% (the relink noise of item 38-adjacent
history); an arm outside the band means the constructor rewrite
changed compiled code and the affected builder is re-examined before
anything else runs.

2.3 PADDING NULL ON BIG SHAPES: padded against newform-unpadded,
interleaved, on vgg and stretch-inner256 arms (~15 min). Lean: flat to
the floor -- padTo is identity above 410 and the branch is one compare
per result allocation.

2.4 THE POINT, ROSTER DE-POISONING AT -A32m: (a) bash poison-scan.sh
32m with the PADDED binary -- lean: the cnn-slice-c32 and
cnn-L1-6x6-c1 rows collapse from +12.2/+9.6% toward the own-group
level task 2.1 measures for their counts; the own-group rows stay on
item 1's curve, which padding cannot touch. (b) The victim-set
remeasure (remeasure.sh's first block, padded binary): the ~+14%
in-roster deflation of the list denominator -- lean: it shrinks by the
