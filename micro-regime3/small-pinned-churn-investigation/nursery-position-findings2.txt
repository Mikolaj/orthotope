    minflt IDENTICAL at n=200 and n=2000 (299,344 = the ~1.17 GB first touch),

29. THE ONE-OBJECT-PER-BLOCK CORNER IS NOT THE INGREDIENT -- any sub-threshold
    pinned churn poisons (ReproSmall modes poisonmid and poisontiny, 2026-08-18,
    repro-matrix2-2026-08-18.txt; same 1.15M object dose as `poison`,
    second-half steady state vs the matrix's alone legs): 1800 B objects (two
    per accumulator block) tax +12.3% at -A32m and +8.8% at -A1G; 800 B objects
    (five per block) +11.8% and +17.5% -- full strength, equal to the 2304 B
    one-per-block spray (+10.8%/+12.1%), where the 3600 B own-group spray stays
    at zero. So the pathway split (shared accumulator pool vs per-object group)
    is the discriminating variable, now with three points below the threshold
    and one above; the block-churn RATE (2-5x fewer blocks at the same object
    count) also did not matter in this range. CONSEQUENCES: (a) plan section
    1.7's "each small object occupies a whole block" clause is refuted
    as the essential ingredient; (b) lowering LARGE_OBJECT_THRESHOLD (e.g.
    to BLOCK_SIZE/2) would NOT eliminate the effect -- objects under the new
    threshold still spray the accumulator path and still poison; it would only
    move the 2048..3276 B window to the (harmless) own-group path,
    a size-window-specific mitigation bought with extra slop for that window;
    (c) the exposure of real programs is broader than micro's two-shape picture:
    ANY Storable vector of <= ~407 doubles sprayed in volume is in the poison
    class; (d) the fix direction that the measurements point at is making
    the accumulator path recycle the way the own-group path evidently does
    (or a separate pinned region), not a threshold move.
